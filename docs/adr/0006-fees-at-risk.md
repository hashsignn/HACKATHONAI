# ADR 6: Fees at risk on the Judgment line only

**Status:** accepted

## Context

Some clients want KPMG to share the outcome, and the team proposed covering a baseline outcome. Pure outcome fees push advisers to tell clients what they want to hear, invite disputes about what success means, and are prohibited for audit clients.

## Decision

An **outcome-linked** option puts a share of the **Judgment** line at risk, never the Signature line. The share is capped by the department's policy pack (20% in Consulting and Deal Advisory). The KPI, its measured baseline, a **threshold** where payment starts and a **target** where it is complete are agreed when pricing is confirmed, along with whose data settles it. Payment ramps linearly between threshold and target and is capped. After the deliverable is signed, the client or manager records the measured value with evidence, and the invoice settles the at-risk part.

## Consequences

- A signed opinion never depends on the outcome it opines on.
- A ramp avoids the all-or-nothing cliff; threshold equal to target is allowed but discouraged.
- Blocked for all of Audit, any audit client, and reports others rely on (financial due diligence).
- KPIs where lower is better (cycle time, cost) are supported.
