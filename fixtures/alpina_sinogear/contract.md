# Supply Agreement (excerpt, SUPPLIER DRAFT v1)

*Fictional document for the UZH.ai Hackathon 2026 prototype. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.*

**Between** Alpina Pumps AG, Winterthur, Switzerland ("Buyer") **and** SinoGear Ltd, Ningbo, China ("Supplier").

**1. Subject.** Supplier shall manufacture and supply custom motor housings, series MH-200, to Buyer's drawings and specifications (Annex A).

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement runs for three (3) years from the Effective Date and renews for successive one-year periods unless terminated under clause 14.

**5.1 Price.** The unit price is USD 48.50 per housing, FCA Ningbo.

**5.3 Price adjustment.** Prices shall be adjusted annually in accordance with Annex C. *(Annex C is not attached to this draft.)*

**6.1 Payment.** Buyer shall pay within sixty (60) days of invoice.

**6.4 Tooling.** Supplier shall procure the dedicated tooling for the MH-200 series at its own cost. Title to the tooling remains with Supplier.

**7.1 Delivery terms.** FCA Ningbo (Incoterms 2020).

**7.3 Delivery schedule.** Supplier shall deliver within a reasonable period after receipt of each call-off.

**8.2 Delay.** For each full week of delay Supplier pays 0.5% of the value of the delayed goods, capped at 2% of that value.

**9. Inspection.** *(No acceptance or inspection procedure is provided.)*

**10.1 Warranty.** Supplier warrants the housings against defects in material and workmanship for six (6) months from delivery.

**11.2 Volumes.** Buyer's forecasts are non-binding. Supplier has no obligation to accept call-offs above its available capacity.

**12.1 Limitation of liability.** Each party's aggregate liability is limited to 10% of the purchases made in the preceding twelve months.

**14.1 Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate on written notice if the other commits a material breach not remedied within thirty (30) days.

**14.2 Termination for convenience.** After the first contract year, either party may terminate for convenience on one hundred and eighty (180) days' written notice.

**18. Governing law.** Swiss law, excluding the CISG. Disputes: ICC arbitration, seat Zurich.

*Annex C (received from Buyer on request): "Supplier may reset unit prices once per contract year at its discretion, with 30 days' notice."*
